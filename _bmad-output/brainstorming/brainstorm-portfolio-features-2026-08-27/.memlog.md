# Brainstorm — Portfolio-Wide Features

## Context / Why
- Home screen (src/app/page.tsx) is currently just a place to click into leagues — no CTA, no insight.
- All platforms now normalize to the same shape (Sleeper live API, Fleaflicker live API, Yahoo DB, MyFFPC DB) → cross-league portfolio reasoning is newly possible.
- Chino manages multiple leagues across 4 platforms. Wants portfolio-level intelligence.

## Seed ideas (user)
- (idea by user) Cross-team roster % — for each player, what % of my teams roster him (exposure).
- (idea by user) Recommended actions per team (surfaced at portfolio level).

## Log

- insight: The core job is TRIAGE, not display — "what's the highest-value action I can take right now, and take me there." Scarce resource = attention across many teams.
- (idea by user) Waiver-wire pickup recommendations per team, driven by analysis Chino provides.
- (idea by user) Overall guidance that applies to SOME leagues but not others (context-dependent rules).
- (idea by user) Want to SEE the recommendations applied (not just abstract advice).
- (idea by user) Strengths & weaknesses of each team at-a-glance.
- insight: Chino provides the analysis (rankings/values) — the system applies it per-league context. Human-in-the-loop, not black box.

- decision: Guidance form = mostly PLAYER-LEVEL (buy/sell certain players), sometimes THEMATIC (trait/cohort), rarely strategic. Maps onto existing rankings/values pipeline — buy/sell is another player tag layer.
- insight: "Buy/sell list" is portfolio-global; the PER-LEAGUE action is derived by combining (a) the tag + (b) that team's context (do I own him? contending vs rebuild? is he a FA here? roster need?).
- technique: started First Principles → wrapping; core primitives identified: [Player buy/sell tags] × [per-team context] → [ranked actions] → [route to the platform to execute].

- technique: started "How Might We" + role-play (personas: Triage Nurse, Exposure Analyst, buy/sell radar).
- idea (coach): ACTION QUEUE — single ranked list of every worth-doing move across all leagues, sorted by value gained, click routes to that league's FAAB/waiver page.
- idea (coach): WAIVER-DAY MODE — home screen knows which leagues process claims tonight, floats those to top with countdown.
- idea (coach): BUY/SELL RADAR — tagged buy players available (FA/weak roster) surface as opportunities; tagged sell players you own surface as "shop these."
- insight (KEY): a buy/sell tag is only actionable RELATIVE to the team's existing roster. "Buy player X" is WRONG advice if all your current players are ranked higher / more valuable. The rec engine must compare the tagged player against the actual roster it'd join, not recommend in a vacuum.
- (idea by user) Format-aware rankings make this possible: dynasty, redraft, and soon in-season rankings — the engine picks the right ranking set per league so buy/sell is scored in the correct context (a redraft buy ≠ a dynasty buy).
- insight: The recommendation is a DELTA, not an absolute — does adding/dropping this player actually improve THIS lineup given format + current roster + roster need? Only surface when the delta is positive and meaningful.

- idea (coach): POSITIONAL HEATMAP GRID — teams × positions, cells colored by strength relative to that league; eye jumps to red = where to act.
- idea (coach): WEAKEST-STARTER per team — lowest-value player in current starting lineup = upgrade target; portfolio-sorted.
- idea (coach): CONTENDER vs REBUILD auto-label from roster age/value curve; makes buy/sell tags read correctly per team.
- direction (user reaction): strengths/weaknesses grid = "valuable, good place to start, but doesn't excite me." TABLE STAKES — ship early/quietly, not where the creative energy goes. Look for the feature that makes Chino lean in.

- technique: started "What Would Delight?" — chasing the lean-in feature, not table stakes.
- idea (coach): BLINDSPOT FINDER — cross-league contradictions (dropped a buy-tagged player in one league who's a FA in another).
- idea (coach): SAME-PLAYER ARBITRAGE — buy him in rebuild league, sell him high in contender league; portfolio has internal trade logic.
- idea (coach): EXPOSURE ALARM — a player you roster across N teams gets injury/role change; roster-% turned into an alert not a chart.
- idea (coach): REGRET PREVENTER — waivers process soon + your worst starter is a sell + you have a claim available.
- idea (coach): WEEKLY PORTFOLIO BRIEFING — digest: buys came open, sells spiked, teams needing lineup attention.
- direction (KEY / user): THE differentiator = analyzing OTHER teams at portfolio scale. Finding trade partners + analyzing the waiver wire across the whole league is what collapses when you manage many leagues. Chino already ingests every roster in every league → opponent analysis is the moat.
- (idea by user) OPPONENT/LEAGUE OPPORTUNITY SCAN — surface advantages & opportunities across each league: who to trade with, what's available on waivers that others are undervaluing.
- (idea by user) IN-SEASON: weekly rankings → LINEUP OPTIMIZER. Highlight WHY a lineup is sub-optimal + the recommended change. Chino may override for his own reasons, but the tool must at least flag it. (Advisory, not autopilot.)

- idea (coach): FIT FINDER — for a team, scan all OTHER rosters for complementary imbalance (they're RB-stacked/WR-thin, you're the mirror); rank opponents by trade-fit score.
- idea (coach): SURPLUS/NEED MATRIX — every team gets a positional surplus/need profile; trade opp = your need ∩ their surplus AND vice versa. League as a matching problem.
- idea (coach): MOTIVATED-SELLER DETECTOR — losing/out-of-contention teams (record + roster age) likely to sell win-now cheap; flag as best trade targets.
- idea (coach): UNDERVALUED FA SWEEP — league-wide, FAs whose your-board value > how the league treats them (still unrostered); the gap = edge.
- idea (coach): DROP-BAIT PREDICTOR — low-value clutter on others' full rosters = likely drops to preempt/grab.
- decision (user): Trade tool should PROPOSE specific players BOTH WAYS, including MULTI-PLAYER deals. Accept rough-at-first, improve over time. Magical > humble. All opponent-analysis ideas above endorsed as "extremely helpful."
- insight: This is the product's heart — OPPONENT ANALYSIS ENGINE (trade proposals + waiver edge) built on the fact that every roster in every league is already normalized. Uses Chino's values to ensure his side nets positive; uses opponent surplus/need to make the deal plausible for them.

- technique: started Assumption-Reversal (challenging trade-engine defaults).
- decision (user, KEY): Trade engine adopts three principles —
  1. DUAL-VALUE LENS: optimize for "looks good to THEM, good for YOU." Model opponent perception via a market/consensus value proxy SEPARATE from Chino's board. Best trade bait = player Chino rates low but consensus rates high. (Requires storing/あccessing a consensus value alongside Chino's own — FantasyCalc value already in DB can serve as the "market" lens vs. Chino's custom rankings as "his board".)
  2. SCARCITY OVER VOLUME: show the SINGLE best trade per league (or a tiny ranked few) with a one-line "why this works for both sides." No 20-deal noise.
  3. TARGET-DRIVEN MODE: Chino names a target player ("get me Travis Hunter here") → engine works backward to find the package THIS owner would plausibly accept, across single + multi-player combos.
- insight: DUAL-VALUE is the unlock — Volatile already has BOTH lenses in the DB (FantasyCalc = market/consensus perception; Chino's custom rankings/values = his edge). Trade bait = positive gap between the two. This is defensible and mostly already-ingested.
- reject (implied): portfolio-level cross-league trade play — not compelling (leagues are siloed for trades).

- technique: started Converging — Impact × Effort (goal = build order).
- decision: Field placed on Impact×Effort. Verdicts agreed with Chino:
  - HIGH IMPACT / LOW EFFORT (BUILD FIRST): G weakest-starter per team; H contender/rebuild label (input for others); R undervalued FA sweep (moat logic in cheapest form — dual-value edge, no combinatorial search); A buy/sell tags (input layer for guidance).
  - HIGH IMPACT / HIGH EFFORT (flagship): O+P+Q trade proposer (the moat); M surplus/need matrix (foundational layer feeding L/N/proposer); T lineup optimizer (gated on in-season rankings).
  - MED (fast follow): C Action Queue (home CTA aggregator, comes after feeders produce output); F heatmap grid (table stakes, ship quietly); L Fit Finder (cheap once M exists); I blindspot + J exposure alarm (roster-% delight).
  - PARK: D waiver-day mode (needs per-league waiver schedules — data cost); E buy/sell radar (subsumed by C+R); K briefing; N motivated-seller (refines M); S drop-bait (refines R).
- decision (user): R (undervalued FA sweep) confirmed as the FIRST real win — moat insight, cheapest form, useful day one.
- decision: Trade proposer MVP = O (dual-value scoring, the brain) + P (scarcity packaging, mandatory to avoid noise); Q (target-driven) is phase 2.
- decision (user): GO — start building the High Impact / Low Effort tier now (G, H, R, A).
