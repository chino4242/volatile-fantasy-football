## General Principles

1. **Don't draft WR in Rounds 1-5.** Exception: Lemon or Concepcion at 1.05 (they're that good).
2. **RB is priority but don't reach.** Coleman is a Round 3 guy. Don't take him at 1.09.
3. **QB by Round 3.** You have zero. Lawrence/Love at 3.01 is the plan.
4. **Rounds 4+ are a flat tier (~1,300-1,450).** Take BPA, don't stress.
5. **Aging vets are fine in Rounds 4-5.** They score points this year and can be traded.
6. **Think in tiers, not names.** If your target is gone, don't reach — take the next tier's best.
